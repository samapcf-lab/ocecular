# OCECULAR by OCOE: partner demonstration

Version 12, 26 October 2026. A fictional prototype for discussion, prepared for the Outreach Centre of Excellence (OCOE).

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

All people, coaches, organisations, courses, listings and media are invented. Conversations and the AI helper are scripted: no AI service is connected, nothing is stored, and no message reaches a real person. Please do not enter real personal details.

The helpline numbers on the Help now screen (999, Childline, Samaritans, NHS 111) are real services. The automatic safety check is a simple word list for illustration: it will miss things and get things wrong, and a real service would never rely on it.

## What changed in version 12

- Elliot leads the demo, with opportunities inside prison and a release checklist that travels in the handover.
- The receiving coach signs in, accepts the handover and sends the first message, then becomes the coach.
- Partner listings carry a minimum age, pay, hours and who can apply; declines need a reason; small counts are banded.

## What changed in version 11

- Safeguarding flags open, get picked up and close with a note; every flag is kept; Ocular stays off until all are closed.
- Coach drafts are kept per young person; replies drafted with Ocular are marked.
- Shared media gets an age rating at review, so adult and custody-made items never reach under-18s.
- Keyboard focus and sticky-bar fixes; clearer duty-to-share wording; the under-18 set-up is not branded as AI.

## What changed in version 10

- Ocular, an AI guide every young person meets first: five tap questions that tailor Home, Learn, Opportunities and Create. Scripted, tap choices only, never decides eligibility, answers private unless shared with the coach.
- Persona profiles for the four example young people.
- A light, calm, readable look (warm off-white, sentence-case headings, no italics) with the colours of OCOE's youth event poster as accents.

## What changed in version 9

- Help now on every screen, with routes for each setting: Childline for under-18s, Samaritans and NHS 111 for adults, staff and Listeners in prison.
- The Coach tab says who can read each conversation, including prison staff while someone is in prison.
- In prison, messages use an approved route with no open internet, and coach visits are requested through the prison.
- Under-18 conversations show supervisor oversight and contact hours; each coach signs in as themselves. The under-18 journey is marked as not designed yet.
- Handovers also cover a care leaver moving area and a pupil moving on at 16.
- The example safety check matches whole words, so everyday words such as "skills" no longer raise a flag, and it catches more ways people describe harm.
- A "Tell us something is wrong" route, a more compact phone layout, and page landmarks for screen readers.

Checked with 189 automated browser checks at phone and desktop widths, including an axe-core accessibility scan. Not tested on a real iPhone, with assistive technology users, or with participants.
